# Hyderabad Traffic-Focused Agentic City Planner

A constraint-aware agentic system for traffic congestion and urban planning in Hyderabad.

## Key Features
- **Citizen Portal**: Multi-modal complaint intake (text, images, videos) for traffic and local hazards, automatically classified and matched to GHMC wards.
- **Planner Dashboard**: Ward-specific dashboards displaying top problems across Traffic (primary), Pollution, and Energy domains.
- **Constraint-Aware Agent**: Proposes realistic infrastructure interventions (signal retiming, rerouting, road widening, drainage upgrades) respecting budget, spatial (Right-of-Way), and timelines.
- **Hyderabad Authority Routing**: Automatic routing of proposed fixes to the responsible Hyderabad department (GHMC, HTP, TSPCB, HMWSSB, TSSPDCL, TSRTC).
